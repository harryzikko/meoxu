## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies warm companionship, non-judgmental guidance, and playful mindfulness. Designed for mobile-first personal financial tracking, it rejects punitive, guilt-inducing banking tropes in favor of an empathetic sanctuary. Instead of rigid spreadsheets, cold sterile grids, or alarmist red warnings, the experience feels like an inviting sunlit cafe where an endearing feline companion gently looks after personal wellbeing. 

The tone of voice is conversational, humble, and supportive: addressing the user as "bạn" and referring to itself as "mình". It focuses on emotional reflection rather than cold discipline: money is framed as time worked and life enjoyed, visualized playfully as dried fish treats ("đĩa cá khô") rather than abstract deficits.

### Visual Style Movement
The visual language merges **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Modern Soft-Minimalism**:
- **Generous Roundedness**: Highly softened radii (24px–32px) evoke safety, organic touch, and friendliness.
- **Warm Chromatic Palette**: Cream bases, toasted chestnut ink, and apricot orange accents replace stark black-and-white digital contracts.
- **Micro-Tactile Depth**: Airy, tinted diffuse shadows mimic warm morning light across textured paper, providing subtle elevation without harsh borders.
- **Emotional Tagging**: Four distinctive pastel accents correspond to psychological states (joy, necessity, regret, impulse), elevating transactions from mere accounting rows into meaningful personal reflections.

## Colors

### Color Strategy & Hierarchy
The palette is built upon a warm domestic spectrum that deliberately excludes pure harsh black (`#000000`) and sterile fluorescent white.

- **Primary (`#FF9F43`) & Secondary (`#FFB26B`)**: Peach apricot tones carrying warmth, optimism, and gentle energy. They mark interactive priorities, floating action prompts, active tab states, and the primary feline spirit.
- **Neutral Base (`#4A3728`)**: Deep toasted chestnut brown serving as the anchor ink. It offers high contrast against cream backgrounds while eliminating the clinical sterility of standard gray or pure black.
- **Canvas Base (`#FFF8F0`)**: A warm, porous cream milk background providing natural calmness to reduce financial anxiety during daily check-ins.
- **Emotional Chromatics**:
  - `emotion-joy` (`#7ED6A5`): Soft mint green indicating joyful, guilt-free spending.
  - `emotion-necessity` (`#8EC5FC`): Sky blue representing foundational, essential costs.
  - `emotion-regret` (`#F28B9B`): Soft coral rose indicating reflective remorse without using accusatory pure crimson.
  - `emotion-impulse` (`#FFD36B`): Sunburst pastel yellow denoting spontaneous or impulsive purchases.

### Dark Mode Adaptation
Dark mode transforms the canvas into roasted espresso brown (`#231B15`) and deep cacao cards (`#2F251E`). Text reverses to warm whipped cream (`#FFF4EA`) and soft oat tan (`#C8B6A6`), retaining emotional hue balance without neon glare.

## Typography

### Type Personality & Vietnamese Diacritics
The typography leverages **Nunito Sans** for its balanced terminal geometry, warm open counters, and native legibility across nested Vietnamese diacritics (dấu ngã, dấu hỏi, dấu nặng). 

- **Display & Currency Stat**: Reserved for the single daily metric ("Hôm nay thoải mái tiêu") and key motivational headlines. The heavy weights (800 and 900) convey certainty, optimism, and warmth without appearing clinical.
- **Headlines & Section Labels**: Rendered in weight 700 to structure daily groups and pet activity logs with pleasant rounded authority.
- **Body Text**: Tuned to weight 500 and 600. Lighter weights (300/400) are intentionally excluded on mobile touchpoints to maintain high contrast and instant legibility under bright sunlight.
- **Work-Hour Reflection Badge**: Scaled to 12px with semibold-to-bold emphasis, turning abstract currency figures into concrete units of personal labor ("≈ 25 phút làm việc").

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The design system operates on a dedicated mobile viewport baseline (nominal width 390px, iOS/Android fluid scaling):
- **Base Canvas Margin**: Fixed outer margin of `1.25rem` (20px) to prevent finger cramp on edge gestures and provide safe padding for floating navigation bars.
- **Column System**: A 4-column fluid responsive system on mobile screens with standard `1rem` (16px) gutters. Elements cleanly span 1, 2, or 4 columns.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Strict multiples of 4px and 8px:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Tag padding, badge inline icon spacing.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between emotion reaction pills and micro-inputs.
  - `space-md` (12px): Standard internal card padding for compact transaction lists.
  - `space-lg` (20px): Structural padding for hero cards, dried fish dishes, and bottom sheet containers.
  - `space-xl` (28px): Section-to-section breathing separation.

### Safe Zones & Bottom Sheet Dock
Because input is optimized for high-speed logging (under 3 seconds), the bottom 88px of the screen is permanently preserved for the Natural Language & Voice persistent docking bar, offset dynamically by device safe area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Sunlit Elevation
Visual depth is defined by **Warm Ambient Diffuse Shadows** combined with tonal surfaces rather than stark drop shadows:
- **Base Canvas (`Level 0`)**: Flat `#FFF8F0`. Non-elevated backdrop.
- **Surface Cards & Lists (`Level 1`)**: `#FFFFFF` cards resting on the cream backdrop, elevated by `box-shadow: 0px 4px 20px rgba(74, 55, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Input Dock & Interactive Cards (`Level 2`)**: Elevated by `box-shadow: 0px 8px 28px rgba(74, 55, 40, 0.08)`. This gives the bottom input bar a soft floating cushion that hovers gracefully above scrolling transactions.
- **Modal Overlays & Emotion Trays (`Level 3`)**: Bottom sheets and quick-tagging drawers with `box-shadow: 0px 16px 40px rgba(74, 55, 40, 0.12)`.
- **Border Treatment**: Clean, low-contrast ghost borders (`1.5px solid rgba(74, 55, 40, 0.06)`) define white surfaces on light cream, keeping elements crisp without visual clutter.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Rounded Radii
The system embraces curved, inviting forms reminiscent of soft cat paws and smooth bakery ceramics:
- **Interactive Buttons & Input Fields**: `rounded-2xl` (16px to 20px) to maximize tactile appeal.
- **Cards, Fish Plates & Hero Modules**: `rounded-3xl` (24px to 28px) delivering prominent physical warmth.
- **Emotion Chips & Quick Tags**: Fully rounded pill forms (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for effortless, natural thumb contact.
- **Feline Mascot Pod**: Housed in asymmetric soft circular cutouts or freeform organic enclosures.

## Components

### 1. Daily Hero Card ("Đĩa Cá Khô & Thoải Mái Tiêu")
- **Container**: White background, `rounded-3xl`, 20px internal padding, Level 1 shadow with a fine oat border (`#FDF2E4`).
- ** dried Fish Plate Graphic**: Centered circular tray displaying stylized flat fish snacks representing remaining daily allowance.
- **Primary Metric**: `currency-stat` formatted with clear thousands separators (e.g., `285.000đ`). Subtitle reading: *"Hôm nay bạn cứ tiêu thoải mái nhé"*.
- **Mascot Integration**: The mascot sits atop or beside the fish plate, dynamically switching Lottie/Rive animations (contented tail wagging, sleeping, or head-scratching curiosity).

### 2. Fast Input Bar (Bottom Dock)
- **Position**: Fixed bottom dock, floating 16px above the device navigation line.
- **Visuals**: Soft pill capsule with Level 2 warm shadow, white fill, and subtle amber glow on focus.
- **Elements**: 
  - Left icon: Natural language text field placeholder: *"Ví dụ: trưa bún bò 45k..."*
  - Right cluster: Voice microphone pill button (`#FF9F43` with white icon) alongside quick number keypad toggle.
  - Parsing Preview Toast: Instant inline pill verifying `[Ăn uống] • 45.000đ` with a subtle 3-second auto-commit progress bar.

### 3. Emotion Reflection Chips
- **Layout**: Horizontal cluster of 4 pill buttons appearing post-transaction:
  - **Vui (Joy)**: Background tint `rgba(126, 214, 165, 0.16)`, border `rgba(126, 214, 165, 0.5)`, text `#2B6A45`.
  - **Cần thiết (Need)**: Background tint `rgba(142, 197, 252, 0.16)`, border `rgba(142, 197, 252, 0.5)`, text `#2A5782`.
  - **Hối hận (Regret)**: Background tint `rgba(242, 139, 155, 0.16)`, border `rgba(242, 139, 155, 0.5)`, text `#8A3140`.
  - **Bốc đồng (Impulse)**: Background tint `rgba(255, 211, 107, 0.20)`, border `rgba(255, 211, 107, 0.6)`, text `#735607`.
- **Interaction**: One-tap selection; frictionless swipe-away option to skip with zero friction or judgment.

### 4. Transaction List Items
- **Structure**: Grouped in daily stacks with a header showing date and total spent.
- **Item Row**: 
  - Left: 44px rounded circular category icon container with pastel tint.
  - Center: Note title in `body-md` bold brown, directly followed by a subline badge: *"≈ 25 phút làm việc"* in `work-hour-badge`.
  - Right: Clean currency amount in `body-lg` accompanied by a small 8px dot representing the selected emotion color.
  - Gestures: Swipe right to edit, swipe left to archive/delete with a 5-second gentle undo snackbar.

### 5. Buttons & Controls
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#FF9F43`, text color `#FFFFFF` (`body-lg` bold), 52px height, `rounded-2xl`, with a light amber underglow.
- **Secondary Button**: `#FDF2E4` surface, text `#4A3728`, borderless, warm hover state.
- **Sliders ("Nếu như..." Simulator)**: Thick 8px rounded track in light cream with a 24px apricot thumb, showing immediate real-time year-end savings comparisons (e.g., *"≈ 1 chuyến đi Nhật"*).

### 6. "Thư của Mèo" (Weekly Insight Cards)
- **Container**: Parchment card layout with organic wavy top header border.
- **Sections**: Non-judgmental narrative prose, one tiny actionable habit experiment, and a warm closing compliment sticker.
